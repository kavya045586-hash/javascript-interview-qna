## Mock Interview Notes

Use this file to log actual mock interview sessions — what was asked, how
you answered, and what to improve.

**Template for each entry:**

```markdown
### Mock Interview #1 — [Date]
**Format:** Self-practice / Friend / Platform (e.g. Pramp, Interviewing.io)

**Questions asked:**
1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Question 3]

**How I answered:**
- [Brief summary of your approach/answer for each]

**What went well:**
- [Things you explained clearly, confidently]

**What to improve:**
- [Topics you fumbled, explanations that were unclear, timing issues]

**Follow-up to review:**
- [Specific concepts to re-study before the next mock]
```

---

**Tips for running your own mock interview:**
1. Pick 5-8 random questions from across your folders (use a random
   number generator on your question count)
2. Set a timer — 2-3 minutes per question, simulating real interview pace
3. Answer OUT LOUD, not just in your head — verbalizing is a different
   skill than recognizing an answer
4. Record yourself if possible — reviewing your own explanation reveals
   filler words, unclear phrasing, or gaps you didn't notice while speaking
